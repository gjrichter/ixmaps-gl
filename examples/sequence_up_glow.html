SEQUENCE · DOWN · GLOW A categorical SEQUENCE|STAR|SORT|DOWN|EXPAND chart with GLOW at four sites: red; red+green; green only; red+blue. The biggest category is the star centre and the others sit around it, so any mix spreads. (With SORT|UP a chart spreads only when every category is non-zero, and in an aggregated categorical cell every absent category is a zero part, so use DOWN for categorical cells.) Every chart glows, on its biggest part.
