UNHCR – people of concern

Forcibly displaced and stateless people worldwide, by country of origin and country of asylum, for every year from 1951 to 2023.

How to read the map

Pies (numbers) – refugees present in a country of asylum; the pie size is the total, the slices are the countries of origin.

Arrows (vectors) – refugees in motion, from the country of origin to the country of asylum; the arrow width is the number of people.

Open circles (outline only, no fill) – people displaced inside their own country (IDPs); the circle size is the number of people.

Colours identify the country of origin. Hover a symbol for the exact figures. The vectors and numbers buttons switch the arrows and the pies on and off.

Timeline

Move the year slider (1951–2023) to see the situation in that year; all symbols are recomputed for the selected year.

Data source

UNHCR Refugee Population Statistics Database, licence CC-BY-4.0. Only a selection of countries is shown.
